## Full Description

Track reps, follow a plan, and see progress.

PushUp Pro makes it easy to start your first workout in seconds and stay consistent every week:

- **Start Fast:** Pick a beginner-friendly plan, set a realistic weekly goal, or skip straight into a workout.
- **Reliable Counting:** Count reps with a quick tap or enable the calibrated hands-free proximity sensor with automatic tap fallback.
- **Weekly Goal Ring:** See how the last 7 days stack up against your weekly target alongside streaks and personal bests.
- **Guided Programs & Offline Logging:** Follow structured sets with rest timers and voice coaching, or log offline workouts with accurate set breakdowns.
- **Custom Schedule & Reminders:** Choose your training days and get timely local reminders when you want them.
- **Optional Sharing:** Celebrate a streak, personal best, or weekly milestone—or send a quick challenge to a friend.

Built to stay private and local on your phone—no account required.
